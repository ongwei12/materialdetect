Every plastic bottle has a story. But YOU get to decide the ending! Play the interactive game below and see how your choices affect the environment.

🏭

Chapter 1: Birth

A brand-new plastic bottle is born in a factory. It's filled with crystal-clear mineral water, ready to be placed on a supermarket shelf. It was made from oil — manufacturing it used 250ml of petroleum.

Chapter 3: The Recycling Path

Because it was sorted for recycling, the bottle goes to a recycling center! Here it gets cleaned, shredded, and melted into pellets. These pellets can become new products.

🚛

Chapter 3: The Landfill Path

Because it wasn't sorted for recycling, the bottle ends up at a landfill — or is incinerated. Burning it releases toxic gases into the air we breathe.

Chapter 4: Nightmare in the Ocean

You littered the bottle! It blows in the wind and flows into rivers, eventually reaching the ocean. Every year, 8 million tonnes of plastic enter the ocean! This bottle drifts for hundreds of years, broken down by sunlight into billions of tiny fragments — microplastics. Fish mistake them for food and swallow them.

🌊

Chapter 4: Into the Ocean

The bottle from the landfill/incinerator or litter eventually reaches the ocean. Every year, 8 million tonnes of plastic enter the ocean! This bottle drifts for hundreds of years, broken down by sunlight into billions of tiny fragments — microplastics. Fish mistake them for food and swallow them.

😱

Chapter 5: Back on Your Plate!

Scientists discovered that humans consume on average the equivalent of a credit card in microplastics every single week! That fish who swallowed microplastics? It ended up on your dinner table…

✨

Chapter 6: A Happy Ending!

Recycled, it could become a new bottle, a T-shirt, a carpet, or even a park bench. One bottle, endless possibilities! 🎉

💡 Quick Tips

Numbers 1 and 2 are the most widely recycled — they can go in the recycling bin in most cities.
Before recycling: Rinse clean, remove the cap (recycle separately), and squash flat to save space!
When in doubt, check your local recycling guide — or simply reduce your use!

📋 Step-by-Step Guide

How to Recycle Plastic Correctly — 5 Easy Steps!

Many people put plastic in the recycling bin — but doing it wrong can mean the whole batch gets rejected.
Follow these 5 steps to make sure your plastic is actually recycled!

🔍
Step 1
Check the Number on the Bottom
Flip the container over and look for the recycling number (1–7). Numbers 1 and 2 can usually go straight into the recycling bin. Numbers 3, 6, and 7 often need special handling — or just put them in general waste.
💡 Take a photo on your phone so you can refer to it later!
🚿
Step 2
Give It a Rinse
Rinse the inside with water to remove food scraps and grease. No need for washing-up liquid — a quick rinse is enough. Containers with food residue can contaminate an entire load of recycling, sending everything to landfill!
💡 It doesn't need to be spotless — just no food left inside!
🏷️
Step 3
Remove Labels and Lids
Most paper labels can stay on. Plastic lids are often a different plastic type — recycle them separately, or check local rules. Foil-lined packaging is usually not recyclable.
💡 Bottle caps are usually PP (#5) — recycle them separately!
📦
Step 4
Squash It Flat!
Crushing plastic bottles can save up to 70% of space in the bin, allowing more to be collected and reducing transport emissions. Just stomp on it or squash with your hand!
💡 Step on it after squashing to keep it flat in the bin!
🗑️
Step 5
Put It in the Right Bin
Place clean, prepared plastic into the dedicated recycling bin (usually blue or green). Never use a plastic bag to collect recyclables — bags jam recycling machinery! If there's a recycling centre nearby, you can drop off directly.
💡 Remember: NOT in a plastic bag — it will jam the machine!
